# Fleet Operator Portal (`apps/portals/fleet-portal`)

> **Role-Based Portal:** Commercial Logistics & Public Transport Fleets  
> **Status:** Architectural Seam (Planned)  

---

## Capabilities & Views
- **Driver Warning Streams:** Real-time push notifications of upcoming potholes and lane obstacles.
- **Route Hazard Analytics:** Corridor risk scores along national freight arteries.
- **Maintenance Risk Index:** Suspension, tire, and rim damage avoidance tracking.
- **Crowdsource Credits:** Monthly rebate accounting based on validated telemetry miles contributed.
