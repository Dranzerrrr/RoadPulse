# Authority Monitoring Portal (`apps/portals/authority-portal`)

> **Role-Based Portal:** Highway Authorities & Concessionaires  
> **Status:** Architectural Seam (Planned)  

---

## Capabilities & Views
- **Incident Queue:** Live triage of corroborated blackspot hazards with video proof.
- **Verification Workbench:** Side-by-side inspection of stationary mast cameras vs passing vehicle dashcams.
- **SLA Workorders:** Automated ticketing for pothole repairs with 48-hour compliance timers.
- **Corridor Heatmaps:** Continuous International Roughness Index (IRI) mapping.
